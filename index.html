Android → Windows over Wi-Fi

Your phone is now your PC microphone.

PhoneMic streams your Android phone's mic to Windows across your home Wi-Fi with about 50 ms of delay. Discord, Zoom, OBS and games see it as a normal microphone.

About five minutes, once

Set up in four steps

  1. Install VB-Cable

    Run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as administrator, then restart. This adds the "CABLE Output" microphone.

  2. Open PhoneMic.exe

    If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway. Allow it on Private networks when the firewall asks.

  3. Scan the QR code

    Point your phone camera at the code in the PhoneMic window. The app opens and connects, or offers itself for download.

  4. Pick the mic

    In Discord, Zoom, OBS or Windows Sound settings, choose CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable).

What you get

Built for low delay

Finds your PC by itself

The app looks for PhoneMic on your Wi-Fi. Pick it from the list, or type the address shown on the PC.

Works with the screen off

Streaming carries on in the background. Mute or stop it from the notification.

Tuned for Wi-Fi

Raw audio over UDP, with Wi-Fi power saving switched off while you stream. A small buffer on the PC keeps the delay from creeping up.

Sound controls

Choose the mic (raw, voice, back camera mic), boost volume up to 400%, and switch on noise suppression or echo cancellation.

Live stats

The app shows estimated delay, Wi-Fi time, PC buffer and lost packets while you talk.

Light and dark

The Windows app follows your Windows theme, or set Light or Dark from the button next to the title.

Troubleshooting

If something doesn't work

The app says "No PC found"

Check that PhoneMic.exe is open, both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and Windows Firewall allowed PhoneMic on Private networks. In Windows, the Wi-Fi network itself should be set to Private. Guest networks often stop devices from seeing each other. You can also tap Enter IP in the app and type the address shown in the PC window.

Android won't install the app

Android asks once to allow installing apps from the app you opened the file with, such as Chrome or Files. Allow it, then tap the APK again. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose More details → Install anyway.

Windows apps don't hear anything

Make sure VB-Cable is installed and you restarted afterwards. In the PhoneMic window, "Send audio to" should say CABLE Input. Your app's microphone should be set to CABLE Output.

The sound crackles or cuts out

Set Packet size to 10.7 ms in the phone app and Delay buffer to 50 ms in the PC app. A 5 GHz Wi-Fi network, or a PC connected to the router by cable, helps the most.

I have an iPhone

There is no iPhone app, but you can use the mic from Safari. Scan the QR code in the PC app and choose to use the browser. Safari will warn about the certificate first; tap Show Details → visit this website. It's your own PC.
